Our story

Automation should expand human potential.

NexusFlow Technologies was founded in 2020 by enterprise software leaders who had spent years watching talented teams lose time to disconnected systems, repetitive handoffs, and brittle automation. They believed there was a better way: a platform powerful enough for IT, approachable enough for operations, and transparent enough for regulated enterprises.

Today, more than 240 NexusFlow team members help organizations in 18 countries automate critical work across finance, customer operations, technology, and people teams. Following our Series B funding, we are investing in responsible AI orchestration, regional infrastructure, and an open ecosystem of integration partners.

Our mission remains straightforward: give every team the power to improve how work moves—without sacrificing security, context, or human judgment.
